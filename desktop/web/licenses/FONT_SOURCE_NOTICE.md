# Bundled font source and copyright evidence

This record accompanies the unchanged 25 bundled WOFF2 files. The original
SIL OFL texts remain in this directory. This supplement preserves notices from
the actual font metadata; it grants no additional permission or release approval.

The per-file hashes, official reference URLs and measured limits are in
[FONT_SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json](FONT_SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json).

## Scope and outstanding conditions

Nine Pretendard files match the official v1.3.9 files and three Gowun files
match the official Google Fonts service binaries. The other thirteen assets
have complete Unicode-mapped outline/metric/hint comparisons against pinned
official TTFs or weight instances. That comparison does not establish their
original generation recipe, full shaping behavior or complete distribution rights.

The eight Nanum assets are character subsets of full reference fonts while
retaining Nanum names. No RFN permission or original subset authority has been
established for those assets. Eight full-font compression alternatives are
preserved only in QA, with original names and all rendering tables preserved.
They have not replaced bundled files or passed native application validation.

Noto Sans KR ExtraLight has metadata weight 400 but mapped glyphs match the
official 200 instance. ExtraLight and Regular have empty .notdef outlines; the
reference .notdef has five contours. Thin/ExtraLight labels also remain in some
Noto internal names. These facts are recorded without changing font data.

This supplement does not replace or remove the original notices, including
the Google copyright in the Noto Serif license and the Adobe copyright carried
by the font. Actual Mac/Hancom/IME/printing and final distribution checks remain
pending; beta publication stays held.
